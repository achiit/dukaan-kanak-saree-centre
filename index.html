Meet the family behind the counter

My father started this shop in 2008 with just one rack of cotton sarees and a borrowed stool. I used to sit beside him after school, learning to feel a fabric before looking at its price tag.

Today, my mother still checks every new lot of silk before it goes on the shelf. If she says no, it does not reach you.

We know most customers by name. Many brides we dressed years ago now bring their daughters. That is the best review we could ask for.
